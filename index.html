About Me

Rohan was born on September 2, 2007, in Berapathalia (Fakir Bari) village, Jamalpur Sadar Upazila, Jamalpur.

His father is Rafiqul Islam Thanda and his mother is Roma Begum.

He completed his primary education from Sreerampur Primary School and his SSC from Sreerampur ML High School

He is currently studying Computer Science and Technology (CST) at Mymensingh Government Polytechnic Institute.

Rohan has been interested in sports since childhood. He particularly enjoys cricket, although he also plays football. He has won prizes in several athletics competitions.

Rohan is also passionate about writing. He has contributed to a joint poetry anthology titled "Bela Sheshe, Kobita". He hopes that more of his poems, stories and novels will be published in the future, Insha'Allah.
