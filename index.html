Rohan was born on September 2, 2007, in Berapathalia (Fakir Bari) village, under Jamalpur Sadar Upazila, Jamalpur. His father is Rafiqul Islam Thanda, and his mother is Roma Begum. He completed his Primary Education Completion (PSC) from Srirampur Primary School and passed his Secondary School Certificate (SSC) from Srirampur Bahumukhi High School. He is currently studying Computer Science and Technology (CST) at Mymensingh Government Polytechnic Institute. From an early age, Rohan has had a strong interest in sports. He particularly enjoys playing cricket, although he also plays football. He has participated in various athletics competitions and has won prizes in several events. Rohan also has a passion for writing. He is a contributor to a joint poetry anthology titled “Bela Shesher Kobita”, in which one of his poems has been published.
